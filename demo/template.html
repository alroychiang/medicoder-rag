Retrieval-augmented ICD-10-CM coding

Before a reimbursement claim can be obtained from an insurance company, standardised ICD-10-CM codes must be attached to the corresponding clinical note. Previously this was done manually, against a codebook of 74,719 billable codes. However, clinical notes are written in informal medical language — “herniated disc” — while the codebook uses standardised terminology — “intervertebral disc displacement”. This prototype bridges that gap automatically: it splits a note into individual conditions, retrieves candidate codes by meaning rather than keyword, then has a language model select the ones that apply.
